# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering leads scanning for a Senior Frontend Developer. They open the page between other tabs, spend 30 to 90 seconds deciding whether to read further, and judge technical depth from the craft of the page itself. Some view on a phone between meetings, others on a large monitor with the page pinned beside a job description.

## Product Purpose

A bilingual personal CV that functions as both a readable resume and a live demonstration of frontend engineering skill. Success means the viewer immediately registers technical competence from the page's execution, then reads the content and reaches out. The site must also export cleanly to PDF via the browser print dialog.

## Brand Personality

Sharp. Meticulous. Technically fluent.

The voice is direct and confident, never boastful. The page should feel like it was built by someone who sweats the details: exact spacing, intentional type, nothing wasted. Warmth comes through precision, not decoration.

## Anti-references

Corporate resume templates, WordPress themes, LinkedIn PDF exports. Anything with generic section dividers, stock icons, or bento-grid layouts that signal "template" rather than "bespoke."

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** The CV is itself a frontend project. Every visual choice must hold up to the same scrutiny the candidate would apply in a code review.
2. **Sharp restraint.** Remove everything that does not earn its place. The result should feel inevitable, not sparse.
3. **Expert confidence.** The design projects authority without trying to impress. No decorative flourishes, no novelty for its own sake.
4. **Bilingual equality.** English and Vietnamese are first-class surfaces, not a translation afterthought. Layout, typography, and spacing work equally well in both.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard responsive design. Mobile-friendly layout with readable text at all viewports. No specific WCAG target beyond reasonable contrast and usable tap targets.
